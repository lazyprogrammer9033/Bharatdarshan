    Vegetarian food guide

    Pure veg, no egg, no fish. Green tag means it is usually made Jain-friendly, with no onion, garlic or root vegetables. Always confirm when you order.

    Each trip is set in its best riding window. Most fit the Canadian winter, so you trade snow for sunshine. The Himalaya trip is the summer one.

    Flights from Canada

    Nonstop options

    Air India and Air Canada fly nonstop between Toronto and Delhi, and there are nonstops from Vancouver too. Other airports connect through Delhi, Mumbai, Europe or the Gulf.

    Fares

    Expect roughly CAD 1,300 to 2,200 return in economy. Fares jump around Christmas and Diwali. Open-jaw tickets (in one city, out another) usually cost about the same as a return.

    Papers to ride

    Get an International Driving Permit from CAA before you fly, and carry your Ontario licence. Rental shops and police will ask for both.

    Permits

    On a Canadian passport (OCI included), Arunachal and parts of Ladakh need a Protected Area Permit. Apply online a few weeks before Trip 8 and Trip 9.

    Fares and schedules change often. Treat these as a starting point and check current prices before booking.
